How to Add Images via ImgBB.com

  1. Visit imgbb.com in your browser.
  2. Click "Start uploading" and choose your profile photo or certificate screenshot.
  3. After upload, copy the "Direct link" (URL ending with .jpg or .png).
  4. Paste that URL into the Image URL field in About Me, Projects, or Certificates below.
